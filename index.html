About

Hello! My name is [redacted] and I am an aspiring VFX artist. I use the program Blender v4.5.3 LTS. I usually prefer being anonymous, using the pseudonym Dimond.

I enjoy creating immersive environments and bringing digital worlds to life through careful attention to lighting, texturing, and animation.

My goal is to create visually stunning and emotionally engaging experiences that transport audiences to new and exciting worlds, with original concepts and attention to detail.

In my free time, I enjoy exploring new technologies and techniques in the field of Blender VFX, as well as learning from other artists to bring my ideas to life.

While working on the timelines for my worldbuilding project/s, I am a perfectionist when it comes to the accuracy and plausibility of the tiniest detail. Usually, that would be a bad thing.

But who knows, maybe my extensive research on particle accelerators just might make Einstein-Rosen bridges, a.k.a. wormholes, a real concept that humans can create in the future.
